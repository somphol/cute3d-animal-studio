# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Cute3D to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and specific. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before rewriting someone's contribution.
- Respect differing opinions and experiences.
- Give credit for ideas, code and art.
- Accept feedback gracefully and apologise when you cause harm.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or unwanted attention.
- Discriminatory jokes, slurs or imagery.
- Sexualized language or imagery in a project for general audiences.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Dismissing reports of harm or retaliating against reporters.
- Adding third-party characters or assets that violate another creator's rights.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chat and any space where someone
represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer at the address on the GitHub profile (`@somphol`). Reports are
handled privately. Consequences range from a private warning to a temporary or permanent ban, at the
maintainer's discretion. Maintainers must remove, edit or reject contributions that violate this code.
